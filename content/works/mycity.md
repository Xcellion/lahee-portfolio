I joined MYCITY, a company specializing in behavior analysis technology through IoT devices, as a Product Designer to redesign their website and app. My goal was to enhance clarity and usability, ensuring their complex services were effectively communicated and user-friendly.

Additionally, I contributed to the design of “MyPlace for Office”, an IoT-powered smart office service that helps employees find optimal workspaces, track colleagues, and manage office resources efficiently.

## Overview and hypothesis

The MYCITY website previously lacked depth and clarity, making it difficult to explain its advanced IoT solutions, including:

- Colleague Location Tracking
- Air Conditioning Control
- Shared Space Congestion Monitoring
- Workplace Analytics / Visualization

**Hypothesis**

- Illustrations would effectively communicate MYCITY’s services while avoiding visual clutter.
- An intuitive interface would improve engagement and comprehension for website and app users.

## Solution

**Website Redesign**

- Used illustrations to simplify complex IoT concepts and enhance visual appeal.

- Developed a comprehensive design system for consistency across platforms.

  <p></p>

**MyPlace for Office App Design**

- Created a user-friendly interface for location tracking, air conditioning control, and congestion monitoring.

- Focused on clear navigation and minimalistic layouts to streamline usability.

  <p></p>

**Enhanced Features**

- Expanded the website with instructional visuals to improve service understanding.
- Integrated real-time data visualization for workplace tracking and optimization.

## Impact and learning

**Impact**

- The redesigned website improved user understanding and client engagement by presenting MYCITY’s services clearly.
- The MyPlace for Office app streamlined employee tracking, gaining positive feedback for its usability and workplace optimization features.

**Learning**

- Simplified visuals, such as illustrations, are effective in explaining complex IoT services.
- User-friendly designs paired with advanced IoT technology improve client trust and platform adoption.
- Integrating user feedback into design strategies ensures seamless, scalable solutions.

This project positioned MYCITY as a leader in behavior analysis and IoT technology, delivering tools that enhance workplace productivity and user experience.
