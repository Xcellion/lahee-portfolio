## Introduction

**Role**\
Art Director, Lead Product Designer\
\
**Project Space**\
E-commerce\
B2C\
Growth Design

\
As the Art Director for Aww, a travel product company, I led product design and art direction to rebuild their design system, enhance the e-commerce site, and establish a cohesive brand identity. The goal was to strengthen their brand image and connect with their target audience while maintaining consistency across all channels and media.

## Overview and hypothesis

The challenge was to unify the brand’s visual identity and improve the user experience of their e-commerce platform to better align with the company’s vision and target audience.

**Hypothesis**

- A consistent and well-defined design direction would strengthen the brand’s identity and improve customer recognition.
- Enhancing the UI/UX of the e-commerce platform would drive higher engagement, traffic, and sales.
- Revamping the social media strategy with cohesive visuals and messaging would boost brand visibility and customer loyalty.

## Solution

**Comprehensive Brand Redesign**\
I redefined the brand’s core design concepts through extensive client discussions, creating a unified system for their logos, fonts, and color schemes. I also crafted a new slogan to reflect the brand’s overarching voice.\
\
**E-Commerce Site Renewal**\
I overhauled the UI/UX of their e-commerce platform, ensuring a seamless and engaging shopping experience that aligned with the new brand identity.\
\
**Social Media Strategy Overhaul**\
I developed a comprehensive social media strategy, updating content and imagery to create a cohesive online presence that aligned with the brand’s voice and vision.\
\
**Art Direction for Advertising**\
I directed advertisement photography and filming, ensuring all visuals consistently reflected the brand’s identity and vision.

## Impact and learning

- Average Order Value 

  **+140%**

- Repeat Visit

  **+120%**

  <p></p>

**Impact**

- **Increased Traffic and Sales**: The redesigned e-commerce site and cohesive branding led to a significant increase in both website traffic and sales.

- **Stronger Brand Identity**: The unified design system became the foundation for all marketing and promotional efforts, strengthening brand recognition and appeal.

- **Enhanced Customer Engagement**: The updated social media strategy and cohesive visuals improved customer engagement and loyalty.

  <p></p>

**Learning**

- **Unified Branding is Key**: Establishing a consistent design system across all platforms and media is critical for strengthening brand recognition.
- **Collaboration Drives Results**: Engaging in detailed discussions with the client ensured the design reflected their vision and goals.
- **Holistic Design Impact**: Aligning the e-commerce platform, social media, and advertising under a unified brand identity creates a seamless and impactful customer experience.

This project highlighted the importance of combining strategic branding with thoughtful design to create a lasting and impactful presence in a competitive market.
