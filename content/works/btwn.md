btwn is an innovative promotional platform transforming referral marketing with a cashback system that benefits both businesses and consumers. By incentivizing word-of-mouth marketing, btwn enhances customer engagement, strengthens brand loyalty, and increases revenue for partner businesses.

As the Lead Product Designer, I led the end-to-end design strategy, overseeing brand direction, corporate identity, UX/UI design, and the development of both the B2B dashboard and B2C app. My role was to create a cohesive and user-friendly experience across all digital touchpoints, ensuring that businesses and consumers could seamlessly interact with the platform.

## Overview and hypothesis

btwn empowers businesses by providing a network that drives sales and customer retention through word-of-mouth referrals. Consumers play an active role in marketing by earning cashback rewards when referring friends.

**How the Service Works**

- Businesses join btwn and create a profile.
- Consumers access these profiles via the app and visit partner businesses.
- Consumers refer friends, and when their referrals make purchases, they earn cashback rewards.
- Automated technology tracks referrals and ensures seamless reward distribution.

**Hypothesis**

- A user-friendly, visually engaging platform will drive participation from both businesses and consumers.
- Word-of-mouth marketing will be more effective in increasing sales and loyalty than traditional paid advertising.

## Solution

***Comprehensive UX/UI Design and Branding***

I designed and implemented a holistic digital experience that seamlessly connected businesses and consumers:

- **B2B Dashboard**: Created an intuitive interface allowing businesses to manage referrals, track customer engagement, and optimize their marketing strategies.
- **B2C App**: Developed an engaging, easy-to-use app that lets consumers participate in referral programs, track rewards, and discover partner businesses.
- **Marketing / Visual Assets**: Designed promotional content, ensuring branding consistency across social media, blogs, and print materials.

**Consumer-Centric Messaging Strategy**

- Established a consistent, user-friendly tone aligned with btwn’s mission of empowering businesses and consumers.
- Developed a seamless onboarding experience to ensure effortless adoption for new users.

## Impact and learning

- Registered businesses

  **+230**

- App MAUs

  **+65%**

- App Downloads

  **5K+**

  <p></p>

**Impact**

- **Revolutionized Referral Marketing**: Empowered consumers to actively engage in marketing while enabling businesses to grow through a trusted referral network.
- **Increased User Engagement**: Simplified UX/UI and visually appealing design encouraged higher participation from both B2B and B2C users.
- **Expanded Growth Potential**: Successfully connected e-commerce and local businesses, unlocking new opportunities for sustainable word-of-mouth marketing.

**Learning**

- **Scalability of Word-of-Mouth Marketing**: Automated referral tracking proved that organic recommendations can be an effective and scalable growth strategy.
- **The Power of Holistic Branding**: Maintaining a consistent visual and messaging strategy across all platforms significantly enhanced trust and usability.
- **User-Centric Design is Key**: Focusing on a frictionless experience was essential to increasing adoption and engagement among businesses and consumers.
