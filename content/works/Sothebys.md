Senior Product Designer, Consultant\
\
**Project Space**\
E-commerce\
B2C\
Growth Design

\
As a Senior Product Designer and Consultant, I joined Sotheby’s product design team to integrate insights from user research, including interviews and analysis, into design strategies. I supported the strategic roadmap and product vision, adapting to the fast-paced auction and luxury goods markets. I also led the entire UX/UI design lifecycle—from research and design to testing and delivery—while ensuring the successful execution of deliverables.

## Overview and hypothesis

Sotheby’s operates in a highly competitive and fast-evolving market, where continuous innovation and platform improvements are essential. The project’s primary goals were to enhance key functionalities such as payments, accounts, and shipping while maintaining consistent UX/UI across all platforms to deliver a seamless, user-friendly experience.

**Hypothesis**

- Integrating user research and usability testing into the design process would result in more user-centric features and increased satisfaction.
- Competitive analysis and stakeholder collaboration would support defining objectives and uncovering improvement opportunities.
- Iterative design and testing cycles would enhance platform usability, boost user engagement, and reinforce the brand’s digital identity.

## Solution

**End-to-End Design Process**

- Led all stages of the UX/UI design lifecycle, including research, wireframing, prototyping, testing, and delivery.
- Collaborated with product managers and stakeholders to define design goals and set clear directions.

**User-Centric Design Execution**

- Developed personas, user journeys, and scenarios to align with user needs and goals.
- Designed low-fidelity system flows, wireframes, and prototypes to quickly validate ideas.
- Delivered high-fidelity UI designs and prototypes tailored to devices and screen breakpoints for development teams to implement.

**Iterative Improvement and Collaboration**

- Analyzed user and stakeholder feedback to refine designs and improve usability.

**Design System Contribution**

- Utilized and contributed to the design system to maintain consistency across platforms.
- Collaborated with design leadership to advance UX practices and foster a design-driven culture within the organization.

## Impact and learning

**Impact**

- **Increased Engagement / Trust**: Improved user satisfaction and retention with intuitive UX/UI, thorough testing, and enhanced features like payments and accounts.
- **Stronger Brand Identity**: Delivered a unified, luxury digital experience across platforms.
- **Scalable Design System**: Streamlined workflows and optimized future product development.
- **Sustained Digital Success**: Despite a decline in overall sales from 2021 to 2023, online sales remained higher than pre-pandemic levels.

**Learning**

- **User Research / Testing**: Reinforced the role of user-centric design in driving effective solutions.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Highlighted how teamwork and iteration boost innovation.
- **Adapting to Digital Shifts**: Showcased the importance of agile digital strategies in maintaining relevance and driving growth in a post-pandemic market.

This project demonstrated how strategic leadership and user-centered design create scalable, impactful digital solutions.
